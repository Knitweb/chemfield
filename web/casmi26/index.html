CASMI 2026 method graph

Which methods did the public notebooks of the CASMI 2026 molecule-identification competition use, which ones go together, and how do the notebooks descend from each other? A signed Knitweb fabric in the ChemField chemistry knowledge field, built from public notebook metadata only.

Method components and how often they appear together

Each circle is a method component. Size = number of notebooks that use it; colour = median public score claim of those notebooks (MRR@25, at least 5 claims). A line means the two components appear together in more notebooks than chance predicts (lift ≥ 1.25, at least 4 notebooks); thicker = stronger association.
